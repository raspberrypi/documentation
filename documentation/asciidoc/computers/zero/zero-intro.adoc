Raspberry Pi *Zero* offers an ultra-compact single-board computer (SBC) design with lower power consumption than our xref:../computers/sbcs.adoc[classic SBCs]. These models omit some features found on classic Raspberry Pi SBCs to achieve a much smaller form factor that's better suited for embedded and portable applications. All Zero models share a common hardware baseline, including:

* A microSD card slot.
* A mini HDMI port.
* Two micro-USB ports, one for input power and one for external devices.

Since version 1.3 of the original Zero, all Zero models also include a mini 22-pin, 0.5 mm (fine) pitch, 11.5 mm width, CSI (camera) port.

The main differences between models within a generation of Raspberry Pi Zero depend on whether the board includes:

* *Wireless connectivity.* Models with a *W* suffix include an on-board radio module for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth; models without the W suffix have no wireless capabilities and require a USB adapter for networking.
* *Pre-soldered GPIO headers.* Models with the *H* suffix or the *with headers* label (depending on generation) come with pre-soldered 40-pin headers. Models without the suffix or label come with unpopulated holes; if you want GPIO access, you need to manually solder the header or install a compatible third-party pin kit.
